# 0043. A stronger design for the app

Status: accepted, 2026-11-24

## Context
The client approved the themes and asked for a stronger mobile design.

## Decision
Within the approved rules (red discipline, signals as shapes, one anatomy): the lead story fills the width with its headline on the picture; top stories are numbered cards that peek and drift; articles open under a full picture with a sheet over it and a reading line in the brand colour; actions float in one pill; categories show their newest picture; pressing gives a little, with light haptics; the five places have a sliding mark. Feeds and sections share one row, so the story comes sooner. Display type goes to 44.

## Alternatives
The web's phone layout, unchanged.

## Consequences
The kicker-then-headline order and the tokens are the same as the web's; the website may take the cover card in M2.
