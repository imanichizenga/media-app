# Mobile accessibility

Checked by tests: every target at least 48, every target named, nothing overflows at twice the text size, contrast of every token pair in every theme, no text in code, reduced motion stops everything that moves.

- A card is one stop. It says category, headline, length, warning, age.
- Counts and positions are said whole. Pictures that repeat the headline are silent; article pictures say their description.
- LIVE and BREAKING are said in words, never by colour.
- Choices say whether they are chosen; tabs say which is open. Removing a saved story is also an action, not only a swipe.
- Player controls do not hide while a screen reader is in use.
- The reader's text size multiplies the phone's.
